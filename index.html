About Us

ExoTechHK Limited is a robotics startup from The Chinese University of Hong Kong focusing on the development of exoskeleton solutions. With a team of professor and researchers, we are dedicated in developing innovative robotic technologies and pushing the application of exoskeletons in the industrial sector. Our goal is to enhance the strength and mobility of workers and reduce the risk of work-related injuries.
